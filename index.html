Frequently asked questions

Know before you choose.

Is Next Chapter a real AT&T offer?

No. This is a proposed concept built for a GTM presentation, not an AT&T product or active promotion.

What happens after the first 12 months?

In the illustrative example, the $20 monthly credit ends and the individual plan returns to $70/month, before any applicable taxes, fees or other changes.

Can I keep my phone number?

Eligible account responsibility transfers generally permit retention of the number. Actual transfer requirements, owner approval, installment obligations and credit evaluation vary.

Will my parents' bill change?

Potentially. The app prototype includes a household impact calculator to show why both account costs need to be evaluated before transferring.
